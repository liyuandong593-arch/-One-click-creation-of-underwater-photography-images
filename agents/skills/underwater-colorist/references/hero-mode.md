# Hero Mode

Hero mode is the cinematic grading mode for underwater images.

It is built on top of a credible Natural correction.

Hero mode should strengthen visual hierarchy, subject presence, depth and atmosphere without breaking realism.

Hero mode is not a filter.

---

## 1. Core Goal

Hero mode should make the image feel:

- more intentional
- more cinematic
- more layered
- more focused
- more visually memorable

while still preserving:

- original composition
- original subjects
- realistic underwater depth
- original light logic
- believable color relationships

The result should feel like:

“A professionally finished underwater hero image from the same original scene.”

---

## 2. Hero Is Built on Natural

Hero mode should start only after Natural correction is credible.

Correct order:

1. preserve content
2. establish Natural baseline
3. improve subject readability
4. clean water
5. recover believable color
6. strengthen local hierarchy
7. refine Hero look

Do not skip directly from raw source to aggressive Hero styling.

---

## 3. What Hero Should Strengthen

Hero mode may strengthen:

- primary subject separation
- visual hierarchy
- midtone readability
- foreground / midground / background separation
- local contrast
- selective color separation
- controlled atmosphere
- cinematic presentation

Hero should guide the eye.

---

## 4. What Hero Does Not Mean

Hero does not mean:

- more blue
- more cyan
- more saturation
- more contrast everywhere
- more sharpening everywhere
- brighter everything
- fake sun rays
- fake volumetric beams
- HDR-like processing
- AI reconstruction

If the image only looks “stronger” but not “better organized,” Hero has failed.

---

## 5. Subject Priority

Hero mode requires a clear primary visual subject.

Possible subjects:

- person
- person + fish
- large marine animal
- coral
- wreck
- structure
- environmental light path

The primary subject should be readable immediately.

Secondary regions should support, not compete.

---

## 6. Person Hero

For underwater people:

Prioritize:

1. face readability
2. skin tone
3. body exposure
4. equipment detail
5. separation from background

### Preferred treatment

- local exposure lift
- better midtone separation
- restrained local contrast
- believable skin recovery
- slightly stronger subject-background separation

### Avoid

- artificial glow around the body
- orange skin
- over-bright face
- background becoming equally bright
- changing anatomy
- changing equipment

The person should look important, not pasted into the image.

---

## 7. Person + Fish Hero

Treat person and fish as layered subjects.

Preferred hierarchy:

- person = primary
- near fish = supporting primary
- mid fish = secondary
- distant fish = atmospheric layer

### Important rules

- preserve fish direction
- preserve fish count
- preserve fish geometry
- do not brighten all fish equally
- do not generate fish

The scene should gain depth, not flatten into one tonal layer.

---

## 8. Large Marine Animal Hero

For whale shark, shark, turtle, ray and other large animals:

Enhance:

- silhouette
- body volume
- scale
- separation from water
- relationship with person

Do not:

- redraw anatomy
- invent texture
- increase animal size
- move the animal
- change distance relationships

The animal should feel more powerful without becoming less real.

---

## 9. Coral / Reef Hero

Hero reef grading may increase separation between:

- cool water
- warm coral
- foreground
- background

### Preferred result

- more readable coral
- stronger color separation
- controlled warm tones
- cleaner water
- clearer structure

### Avoid

- aquarium colors
- excessive orange
- excessive yellow
- global saturation boost
- unrealistic red recovery

Hero reef should feel rich, not artificial.

---

## 10. Water in Hero Mode

Water should feel:

- clean
- deep
- layered
- visually controlled

Do not simply make it darker or bluer.

Hero water grading may use:

- selective density
- local haze control
- top-water cleanup
- foreground/background separation

Preserve realistic distance falloff.

---

## 11. Top-Water Hero Treatment

The upper water region often carries atmosphere.

Hero may:

- reduce dirty gray haze
- improve blue density
- preserve surface brightness
- strengthen smooth transition to mid-water

Do not create:

- dark-blue ceiling
- fake sky
- hard tonal band
- artificial light source

---

## 12. Midtone Hero Treatment

Midtones are a key Hero tool.

Hero should often improve:

- person readability
- fish visibility
- reef structure
- subject-background separation

Use midtone shaping before aggressive exposure or contrast changes.

Hero images often feel stronger because midtones are organized better.

---

## 13. Shadow Treatment

Hero may preserve deeper shadows than Natural mode.

But important subjects must remain readable.

Preferred result:

- darker secondary regions
- readable primary subject
- strong tonal hierarchy
- believable underwater depth

Avoid crushed subject blacks.

---

## 14. Highlight Treatment

Highlights may be used as visual anchors.

Protect:

- surface light
- bubbles
- reflections
- equipment highlights
- artificial-light hotspots

Hero highlights should feel controlled and intentional.

Do not blow them out just for drama.

---

## 15. Artificial Light in Hero Mode

Hero can strengthen existing light.

It cannot invent new light.

Allowed:

- reinforce original light direction
- improve local light falloff
- slightly increase subject visibility within the existing beam

Not allowed:

- create new sun rays
- create fake beam direction
- illuminate objects that were not lit
- create large artificial glow

---

## 16. Deep-Water Hero

Deep-water Hero should preserve darkness.

Preferred qualities:

- stronger subject hierarchy
- cleaner dark water
- readable midtones
- controlled local light
- realistic depth

Key rule:

Darker but clearer is better than brighter but fake.

---

## 17. Color Separation

Hero may strengthen selective color contrast.

Examples:

- cool water vs warm coral
- cool water vs believable skin
- dark background vs brighter subject
- neutral equipment vs cyan water

Do not create color relationships unsupported by the original source.

---

## 18. Sharpness

Hero may use stronger selective sharpness than Natural.

Priority:

- face
- primary subject
- near fish
- coral detail
- important structural edges

Keep distant regions softer.

Do not sharpen the whole frame equally.

---

## 19. Hero and AI Editing

Generative AI should be used conservatively.

Hero style does not justify:

- changing content
- regenerating fish
- modifying faces
- changing light sources
- inventing textures
- reconstructing coral

If Hero styling conflicts with content preservation, content preservation wins.

---

## 20. Hero QC

Before approval, confirm:

### Content

- composition unchanged
- no new objects
- no missing objects
- no fish-direction changes
- no fish-count changes
- no anatomy changes
- no geometry changes

### Subject

- primary subject is immediately readable
- subject stands out naturally
- local correction has no halo
- skin remains believable

### Water

- water is cleaner
- depth remains realistic
- no excessive blue
- no over-dehaze
- top-water transition is natural

### Light

- original light direction preserved
- no fake beam
- no fake sun ray
- highlights controlled

### Tone

- midtones organized
- shadows support hierarchy
- highlights support hierarchy
- image is not globally over-contrasted

### Texture

- no over-sharpening
- no plastic skin
- no invented fish detail
- no obvious AI artifacts

---

## 21. Hero Pass Condition

Hero mode passes when:

- the image has a clear visual center
- the primary subject is stronger
- the water is cleaner and deeper
- the scene has better spatial hierarchy
- the image feels more cinematic without looking synthetic
- the viewer notices the photograph before noticing the processing

Hero should feel intentional, not artificial.
